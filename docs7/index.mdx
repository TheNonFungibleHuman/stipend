Stipend gives an AI agent its own wallet and a spending policy that is enforced **between the price
quote and the signature**. The agent buys what it needs one call at a time; each purchase settles on
Solana devnet in about a tenth of a second and leaves a receipt anyone can open in an explorer.

</Info>

**Console: https://stipend-delta.vercel.app** — that is the address to use everywhere below.

## The two guarantees

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A refusal is not a failure" icon="shield">
    The policy runs before the transaction is built. A blocked purchase is a signature that was
    never produced, and the record says why.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settlement is the authorisation" icon="receipt">
    The vendor re-reads the payee's balance on chain before it returns a byte. There is no account,
    no API key, and no billing table to reconcile.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What it is not

- **Not a router.** Nothing picks a vendor for you. Your agent decides what to buy.
- **Not your model provider.** DeepSeek, OpenAI and Anthropic already bill per call and already cap
  spend. Stipend sits below them, on the vendors that will not sell you one call.
- **Not a custodian of value.** The demo stablecoin (`dUSD`) is minted by the treasury and worth
  nothing.

## When you do not need it

If your agent only ever calls one vendor, that vendor already bills per call, and its own spend
limit is enough — keep what you have. Stipend earns its place when the vendor count rises, when a
vendor will not sell you a single call, or when you want one budget and one audit trail across all
of them.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | A keypair, a token account, two keys, and a policy |
| **Vendors** | Paid endpoints that answer `402` without proof and `200` after verification |
| **Wallet service** | `/api/agents/:id/authorize` — the only place a signature exists |
| **Console** | Status, runs, limits, ledger, and the setup prompt for your own agent |
