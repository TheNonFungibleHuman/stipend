---
title: Best practices
description: How to set limits, read refusals, and keep a demo — or a fleet — from going wrong.
---

## Set limits you would be comfortable losing

The right daily budget is the amount you would not mind losing to a bug. The right per-call cap is
the most you would pay for a single answer without checking.

| Situation | Per-call | Daily |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trying the product | $0.10 | $1.00 |
| A working agent | $0.25 | $10.00 |
| Budgeted monitoring | $0.50 | $50.00 |

There is no ceiling imposed by Stipend. There is also no safety net beyond the caps you set: an
agent with a $500 daily budget and a broken loop will spend $500.

## Treat a refusal as data, not an error

A refused call means the policy did its job. The reason names the exact limit. Two rules:

1. **Never retry a refusal.** It will be refused again; a retry loop is how you turn one bug into
   hundreds of ledger rows.
2. **Report it to whoever set the limit.** The refusal reason is written for a human.

## Use the allowlist when the vendor list is known

An allowlist turns "any vendor" into "these three vendors, forever". It is the cheapest protection
against a prompt-injected agent deciding to buy from somewhere new.

## Ask for status before spending

`agent_status` is free. An agent that checks its budget before buying avoids two failure modes:
surprise refusals and a half-finished task it cannot pay to complete.

## Do not top up reflexively

If an agent keeps running out, the fix is usually a smaller task or a higher cap — not a bigger
wallet. A wallet that refills itself hides the signal you need.

## Keep the view key for sharing

The owner key can move money. Put it in the agent's environment and nowhere else. Use the view key
for dashboards, screenshots, and teammates.

## Watch settle time as a health signal

Median settle time of 100–300ms is normal on devnet. A run of multi-second confirms usually means
the public RPC is throttling; a dedicated RPC endpoint fixes it.

## Prove the refusal in a demo

Nothing communicates the product faster than lowering the cap mid-demo and showing a purchase
refused with a reason and no signature. Do it in every demo — it is the whole thesis in one click.
